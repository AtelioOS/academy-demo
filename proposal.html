Executive summary

A growth engine, not just a training portal

Your biggest constraint isn't effort — it's your calendar. The 15–20 hours your leaders spend one-on-one are the real ceiling on how fast the team can grow. This platform lifts that ceiling: people plug into a system and come out ready to produce — grounded in the higher-level concepts your top performers teach — so you get your time back to work on the business instead of in it.

Today, your training lives in scattered pages, your recruiting has no system behind it, and your best people spend their hours repeating the same lessons instead of building the business. We propose a single platform that turns your team's development into a repeatable system: recruits come in, move through structured training and licensing largely on their own, and your leaders step in only where their time truly matters. It follows one connected path — recruit → train → license → produce — built on Atelio, so it grows with you and never locks you into a rigid template.

Scope

What's in — and what we're deliberately leaving out

Keeping scope tight keeps your cost down and your platform focused. Some things you already have handled elsewhere, so we won't rebuild them.

In scope
  • Agent training — Lower Laws + Higher Laws, beginner→advanced
  • Field-training track — ride-along & Zoom sessions, standardized
  • Licensing prep — 4 modules, quizzes, flashcards, progress tracking
  • Contests & recognition — leaderboards, bonuses, trip incentives
  • Recruiting CRM + automated outreach
  • Public event calendar with auto-promotion
  • AI social media engine (later phase)
Out of scope — already handled
  • Client-side portal — handled by the fund companies
  • Commission tracking — handled by WFG (EchoSoft / MyWFG)

We flag these on purpose: you shouldn't pay to duplicate systems WFG and your carriers already provide. It keeps the build lean and the monthly fee low.

Funding & cost offsets

Ways to bring the real cost down

Between a training-focused grant, financing, and the subscriptions this replaces, the net cost can be meaningfully lower than the sticker price.

Grant · strongest fit

B.C. Employer Training Grant

Covers 80% of training costs, up to $10,000 per employee per year (max $300,000 per employer). Self-employed people qualify if operating in B.C. for 1+ year. Because this platform is fundamentally about training your agents, it's the most natural fit — worth exploring with a grant advisor to structure it correctly.

Financing & others
  • BDC financing — interest-free / low-interest options for digital projects (the CDAP-linked $100K loan stream)
  • Regional / startup grants — several BC small-business programs list annually; availability rotates
  • The ~$200K precedent (per Nima) was likely a stacked / CDAP-era package — worth asking him which programs
Subscriptions this can replace

The platform folds in tools you pay for separately today — so part of the monthly fee is offset by cancellations: DocuSign (flagged as high-cost), Calendly, and scattered website/hosting for the training pages. We'll tally the exact savings against your current bills.

Note on grants: government programs open, close, and change eligibility frequently. Figures above are current as of this proposal; we'll confirm live status and eligibility before you count on any single program. (Forma helps you find and apply — we're not a licensed grant consultant.)
